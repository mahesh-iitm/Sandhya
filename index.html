Kāla divisions follow sandhyakala.sringeri.net. Prātaḥ: Uttama from 48 min before sunrise to dawn (Sun 6° below horizon), Madhyama to sunrise, Adhama to 72 min after sunrise. Mādhyāhnika: Madhyama from 264 min after sunrise (or 96 min before noon), Uttama the 48 min before solar noon, Adhama to 72 min after. Sāyaṁ: Uttama the 48 min before sunset, Madhyama to nautical dusk (Sun 12° below), Adhama to 72 min after sunset. Prāyaścitta runs to 3 hours after sunset. Brāhma Muhūrta is 96 to 48 min before sunrise. Sunrise and sunset are apparent times, with geometric times in brackets.
